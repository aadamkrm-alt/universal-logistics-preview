Freight service — Dubai

Air freight from Dubai

International air freight, the default mode for compact high-value cargo where value and urgency outweigh unit freight cost.

Overview

For cargo that is small, valuable and time-sensitive, air is not a premium option — it is the only sensible one. The freight cost per unit on a consignment of handsets is trivial next to the capital tied up while it is in transit.

Universal books and documents air movements from Dubai, working from a facility inside the free zone immediately alongside the airport.

What is included

What air freight covers

  • Booking and carrier selection

    Space booked against the routing, deadline and handling requirements of the consignment rather than the cheapest available rate.

  • Export documentation

    Air waybill, commercial documentation and any declarations the routing requires, prepared and checked before departure.

  • Dangerous goods handling

    Consignments containing lithium cells or batteries packed, marked and declared to the applicable requirements.

  • Consolidated and direct movements

    Consolidation where volume and timing suit it, direct booking where the consignment justifies its own movement.

  • Airport-to-airport and door delivery

    Movement to the airport of destination, or through to the consignee’s address where instructed.

  • Handled with the specialist services

    Where the consignment has been scanned, held or repacked by Universal, the same reference and record carries through into the air booking.

Dubai context

Working alongside the airport

Dubai Airport Free Zone adjoins Dubai International, one of the busiest air cargo airports in the world, with the route density that comes with it. For a trader, that breadth of destination coverage is the practical reason stock is routed through Dubai in the first place.

The short distance between facility and aircraft matters more than it sounds. Consignments that have been received, scanned, held and repacked in the free zone reach the airport without a long domestic road leg, which for high-value cargo removes both time and exposure.

Because the free zone sits outside the UAE customs territory, cargo can be flown in, handled and flown out again as a re-export without a domestic import.

Scope and limits

  • Transit times depend on routing, carrier schedules and clearance at destination. Quoted times are estimates, not guarantees.
  • Lithium batteries are regulated in air transport. Consignments containing them are accepted only where they can be correctly packed, marked and declared.

Common questions

Air freight — questions we are asked

Can phones be shipped by air?

Yes. Mobile phones and other lithium-battery devices move by air routinely, provided the consignment is packed, marked and declared to the applicable dangerous goods requirements.

Can you arrange air freight for goods you are already holding?

Yes. Where Universal has received, scanned or held the consignment, release and the outbound air booking are handled as one job under the same reference.

Do you handle general cargo as well as electronics?

Yes. Air freight is available for general cargo alongside the specialist electronics work.
