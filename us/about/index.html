About Universal Freight & Logistics LLC

The Americas gateway of the Universal network — receiving, holding and distributing high-value technology cargo from Doral, Miami.

About Universal

What Universal does in Miami

Universal Freight & Logistics LLC operates from Doral, minutes from Miami International Airport, handling mobile phones, high-value electronics and general freight.

The Miami operation sits at the distribution end. Consignments arrive from Asia and the Gulf as single lots and are received, counted, scanned and stored. They then leave as separate allocations to buyers across the Caribbean, Central America and South America.

It also works in the other direction, as the origin point for US-sourced stock moving to Universal’s operations in Dubai and Hong Kong.

Why the Miami position matters

Miami International handles more United States–Latin America air freight than any other US gateway, which is why Doral exists as a logistics district at all.

For a distributor, that concentration means one inventory position can serve a dozen markets on short lead times, instead of every regional buyer importing separately from Asia.

The network

Universal Freight & Logistics LLC is one of three connected operations, alongside Universal Logistics FZE in Dubai Airport Free Zone and Universal Freight (HK) Ltd in Kwai Chung, Hong Kong.

Each is a separate legal entity in its own jurisdiction. On the Dubai and Hong Kong lanes, Universal handles both ends of the movement.

Scope

Exactly what Universal does — and what it does not

Stated plainly so nothing is discovered late. If a service you need is on the right-hand list, it is better to know now.

What Universal does

  • Receives, counts and verifies consignments against documentation
  • Captures IMEI and serial numbers and issues reconciliation files
  • Holds cargo in secure storage under a recorded release authority
  • Allocates and partially releases stock to multiple onward buyers
  • Repacks, relabels and prepares consignments for onward carriage
  • Books, documents and moves cargo by air, sea and road
  • Coordinates customs formalities and prepares supporting documentation

What Universal does not do

  • Test, benchmark, grade, repair, refurbish, unlock or activate devices
  • Check identifiers against manufacturer, carrier or law-enforcement databases
  • Hold, transmit or escrow funds, or take any part in commercial settlement
  • Alter, obscure or falsify customs declarations or transport documentation
  • Remove or alter IMEI numbers, serial numbers or regulatory markings
  • Provide legal, tax, duty classification or regulatory advice
