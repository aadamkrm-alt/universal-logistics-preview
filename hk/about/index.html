About Universal Freight (HK) Ltd

The origin-end operation of the Universal network — consolidating, verifying and exporting high-value technology cargo from Hong Kong.

About Universal

What Universal does in Hong Kong

Universal Freight (HK) Ltd operates from Kwai Chung, in the middle of Hong Kong’s cargo geography, handling mobile phones, high-value electronics and general freight.

The Hong Kong operation works at the start of the chain. Stock arrives from suppliers across Asia and from across the boundary in the Pearl River Delta, frequently in small lots against a single order. It is received, reconciled against that order, counted, scanned, inspected and repacked into one shippable consignment.

From there it is exported — most often to Dubai or Miami, where Universal receives it at the other end.

Why origin-end handling matters

Checking a consignment is worth most where a problem is still solvable. In Hong Kong, a short count or a wrong variant is a conversation with a supplier a short distance away. After export, the same finding becomes a claim against a party in another country.

That is the argument for doing the verification work before the cargo leaves Asia rather than after it arrives somewhere else.

The network

Universal Freight (HK) Ltd is one of three connected operations, alongside Universal Logistics FZE in Dubai Airport Free Zone and Universal Freight & Logistics LLC in Doral, Miami.

Each is a separate legal entity in its own jurisdiction. On the Dubai and Miami lanes, Universal handles both ends of the movement.

Scope

Exactly what Universal does — and what it does not

Stated plainly so nothing is discovered late. If a service you need is on the right-hand list, it is better to know now.

What Universal does

  • Receives stock progressively and reconciles it against an order
  • Consolidates multi-supplier consignments into a single shipment
  • Captures IMEI and serial numbers and issues reconciliation files
  • Inspects packaging, specification, condition and seal integrity
  • Holds cargo in secure storage under a recorded release authority
  • Repacks, relabels and prepares consignments for export
  • Books, documents and moves cargo by air, sea and road

What Universal does not do

  • Test, benchmark, grade, repair, refurbish, unlock or activate devices
  • Check identifiers against manufacturer, carrier or law-enforcement databases
  • Hold, transmit or escrow funds, or take any part in commercial settlement
  • Alter, obscure or falsify customs declarations or transport documentation
  • Remove or alter IMEI numbers, serial numbers or regulatory markings
  • Provide legal, tax, duty classification or regulatory advice
