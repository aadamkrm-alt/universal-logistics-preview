Specialist handling

Built around how the phone trade actually works

A consignment of handsets is not a pallet of freight. It is compact, extremely valuable, individually serialised, and very often sold while it is still sitting in a warehouse waiting for someone to say it can move.

That creates requirements a general forwarder is not set up for: proving exactly what is in the cartons, holding stock securely while a transaction settles between parties in different countries, preparing it so it does not disclose its commercial history, and only then moving it.

Universal has organised its Dubai operation around those requirements rather than treating them as exceptions.

How it works

Receive, verify, scan, hold, prepare, release, forward

The sequence a consignment actually goes through, and the point at which each decision sits with you.
  1. 01

    Receive

    Cargo arrives at the facility and is booked in against your reference. Cartons are counted and weighed, and the condition of every seal is recorded.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Piece count, model, specification and carton contents are checked against the packing list and invoice. Discrepancies are reported to you the same working day.

  3. 03

    Scan and inspect

    IMEI and serial numbers are captured and a physical inspection is carried out. You receive the scan file as a record of exactly what is in your stock.

  4. 04

    Hold and store

    Stock sits in secure storage under your name. Nothing moves and nothing is released until an authorised instruction is received.

  5. 05

    Neutralise and repack

    Where instructed, shipping marks and prior references are removed or replaced, cartons are relabelled and the consignment is repacked for its next leg.

  6. 06

    Release

    On your written instruction the consignment is released — to your buyer, to a collecting agent, or into the next stage of forwarding.

  7. 07

    Forward

    Booking, documentation and carriage to the destination, with the same reference carried through from receipt so the whole job stays traceable.

Beyond phones

The same handling across high-value technology cargo

Phones are the clearest case, not the only one. Tablets, laptops, wearables, components and accessories share the same profile — small, valuable, serialised, easy to move — and need the same discipline.

Everything serialised can be scanned and reconciled. Everything valuable is stored under controlled access and released only on instruction.

  • Secure receipt and piece count

    Consignments are booked in against your documentation, counted at carton and unit level, and any variance is reported to you before the stock is put away.

  • IMEI and serial capture

    Device identifiers are scanned and recorded so your stock file, your supplier’s file and the physical cartons can be reconciled against one another.

  • Physical inspection

    Cartons and units are checked for condition, seal integrity, model and specification against what the paperwork says should be there.

  • Hold pending release instruction

    Stock is held in secure storage and moves only when the authorised party instructs release — the mechanism that lets a trade settle before goods travel.

Common questions

Questions we are asked

What does Universal Logistics specialise in?

Logistics for mobile phones and high-value electronics: secure receipt, IMEI and serial scanning, physical inspection, hold and release, neutralisation and repacking, secure warehousing, and international air, sea and road freight. Conventional freight forwarding is also offered.

Where is Universal Logistics FZE based?

In Dubai Airport Free Zone (DAFZA), United Arab Emirates, with connected operations in Kwai Chung, Hong Kong and Doral, Miami.

Does Universal provide IMEI scanning?

Yes. IMEI and serial numbers are captured across a consignment and issued as a data file for reconciliation against your own and your supplier’s records.

Does Universal offer hold and release?

Yes. Cargo is received and held in secure storage, and released only on the written instruction of the party recorded as the release authority. It is a cargo custody service and involves no handling of funds.
