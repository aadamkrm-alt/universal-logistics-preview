Secure warehousing in Miami

Storage for compact high-value stock, with accurate inventory records and release only on authorised instruction.

Overview

In Miami, warehousing is mostly a distribution function. Stock arrives as one consignment and leaves as many, drawn down by buyers across the region as they confirm.

The Doral facility holds that inventory accurately and releases it in the allocations you instruct.

In one line

Storage for compact high-value stock, with accurate inventory records and release only on authorised instruction.

What is included

What secure warehousing covers

  • Receipt and put-away

    Stock is booked in against your reference, counted, and placed into store with its location recorded.

  • Inventory records

    What is held, in what quantity, under whose name, is recorded and can be reported to you.

  • Controlled access

    Goods are released only against authorised instruction, never on a verbal request or to an unnamed party.

  • Stock movements

    Pick, allocation, partial release and transfer between consignments handled against written instruction.

  • Value-added handling in store

    Scanning, inspection, relabelling and repacking carried out on stock while it is held, without a separate movement.

  • Onward despatch

    Release directly into an outbound booking, so storage and forwarding stay one job under one reference.

Miami context

Inventory for regional distribution

Serving buyers across the Caribbean and Latin America from a single US position means holding stock that is committed to nobody until it is allocated.

That requires inventory accuracy at unit level rather than pallet level, because allocations are drawn in small quantities and frequently across product categories.

Being minutes from Miami International Airport means an allocation confirmed in the morning can be on a southbound flight the same day, so stock does not have to sit long once it is released.

Scope and limits

  • Storage terms, duration and rates are agreed per consignment. Rates depend on volume, duration and the handling involved.
  • Insurance is not automatic. Cover for stored goods is agreed separately and should be confirmed before stock is delivered.
  • Specific security arrangements, accreditations and facility certifications are confirmed on enquiry rather than described generically here.

Common questions

Secure warehousing — questions we are asked

Can stock be allocated to buyers in different countries?

Yes. Held inventory can be broken into separate allocations and released for onward movement to individual buyers across the region.

What inventory reporting is available?

Holdings recorded against your reference can be reported, including quantities received, allocated and remaining.

Is insurance included in storage?

No. Cover for stored goods is arranged separately and should be confirmed before stock is delivered to the facility.

Can you receive stock directly from the airport?

Yes. Cargo arriving at Miami International can be collected and delivered into the Doral facility as part of the same job.
