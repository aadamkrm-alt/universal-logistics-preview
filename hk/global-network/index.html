Global network

Hong Kong, Dubai and Miami

Three operations positioned where the high-value electronics trade physically routes — with Hong Kong at the origin end of the chain.

Network map

How the network works

Both ends of the lane

Most of the stock Universal handles begins its journey in or near Hong Kong. It is consolidated, checked and exported from Kwai Chung, and much of it is received again by Universal at the other end.

That continuity is the point of operating in three places rather than one. The record made at origin is the record used at destination.

Common questions

About the network

Are the three offices the same company?

They are three separate legal entities operating under the Universal name in their own jurisdictions: Universal Freight (HK) Ltd, Universal Logistics FZE in Dubai and Universal Freight & Logistics LLC in Miami.

Does the scan record follow the cargo?

On lanes between Universal’s own facilities, yes — the consignment reference and the identifier record carry through to the receiving office.

Can you export to destinations outside the network?

Yes. Air and sea exports are arranged worldwide. The three offices are the points at which Universal handles the cargo directly.
