About Universal Logistics FZE

A logistics operation built for compact, high-value, individually serialised cargo — and for the way that cargo is actually traded internationally.

About Universal

What Universal does

Universal Logistics FZE operates from a facility inside Dubai Airport Free Zone, handling mobile phones, high-value electronics and general freight for international traders, wholesalers and distributors.

The work divides into two connected halves. The first is specialist handling: receiving consignments, verifying and recording what is in them, holding them securely under controlled release, and preparing them for onward movement. The second is conventional freight forwarding: booking, documenting and moving cargo by air, sea and road.

Most customers use both, because for this kind of cargo they are not really separable. The value of a scan record is that it travels with the consignment; the value of holding stock is that it can be released straight into a booking.

Why the specialisation exists

The wholesale phone and electronics trade has characteristics that general freight handling does not accommodate well. Goods are frequently sold while in storage, between parties in different countries who have not met. Every unit carries a unique identifier, and the difference between 498 and 500 units is a serious sum of money. Consignments routinely need to be prepared so they do not disclose a trader’s supplier to their customer.

None of that is exotic within the trade. It is simply not what a general forwarder is organised to do. Universal is organised to do it.

The network

Universal operates in three locations that correspond to how this trade physically routes: Hong Kong at the source, Dubai as the re-export hub between Asia, Africa, the Gulf and the CIS, and Miami as the gateway to the Americas.

Each is a separate legal entity operating in its own jurisdiction. Where a consignment moves between two of them, it is handled by Universal at both ends under the same reference.

Scope

Exactly what Universal does — and what it does not

Stated plainly so nothing is discovered late. If a service you need is on the right-hand list, it is better to know now.

What Universal does

  • Receives, counts and verifies consignments against documentation
  • Captures IMEI and serial numbers and issues reconciliation files
  • Inspects packaging, specification, condition and seal integrity
  • Holds cargo in secure storage under a recorded release authority
  • Removes prior shipping marks, relabels and repacks for onward carriage
  • Books, documents and moves cargo by air, sea and road
  • Coordinates import, export and free zone re-export formalities

What Universal does not do

  • Test, benchmark, grade, repair, refurbish, unlock or activate devices
  • Check identifiers against manufacturer, carrier or law-enforcement databases
  • Hold, transmit or escrow funds, or take any part in commercial settlement
  • Alter, obscure or falsify customs declarations or transport documentation
  • Remove or alter IMEI numbers, serial numbers or regulatory markings
  • Provide legal, tax, duty classification or regulatory advice
