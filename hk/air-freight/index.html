Air freight from Hong Kong

International air freight, the default mode for compact high-value cargo where value and urgency outweigh unit freight cost.

Overview

Hong Kong International is among the world’s largest air cargo airports, and for electronics leaving Asia it is the default departure point. Route coverage, frequency and handling capacity are all there.

Universal books and documents air exports from its Kwai Chung facility, a short run from the airport.

What is included

What air freight covers

  • Booking and carrier selection

    Space booked against the routing, deadline and handling requirements of the consignment rather than the cheapest available rate.

  • Export documentation

    Air waybill, commercial documentation and any declarations the routing requires, prepared and checked before departure.

  • Dangerous goods handling

    Consignments containing lithium cells or batteries packed, marked and declared to the applicable requirements.

  • Consolidated and direct movements

    Consolidation where volume and timing suit it, direct booking where the consignment justifies its own movement.

  • Airport-to-airport and door delivery

    Movement to the airport of destination, or through to the consignee’s address where instructed.

  • Handled with the specialist services

    Where the consignment has been scanned, held or repacked by Universal, the same reference and record carries through into the air booking.

Hong Kong context

Exporting from Asia’s air cargo hub

Most electronics consignments leaving Hong Kong fly, and most of them are consolidated before they do. Bringing multi-supplier stock together into one booking is usually the difference between a workable freight cost and an uneconomic one.

Hong Kong’s free port status means export movements are not encumbered by duty considerations on the way out, which keeps the process to a documentation and booking exercise rather than a customs negotiation.

Where Universal has already consolidated, checked and repacked the consignment at Kwai Chung, the air booking follows directly from that work rather than starting again with a new party.

Scope and limits

  • Transit times depend on routing, carrier schedules and clearance at destination. Quoted times are estimates, not guarantees.
  • Lithium batteries are regulated in air transport. Consignments containing them are accepted only where they can be correctly packed, marked and declared.

Common questions

Air freight — questions we are asked

Can you consolidate several suppliers into one air shipment?

Yes. Consolidating multi-supplier stock into a single booking is one of the most common reasons cargo is routed through the Kwai Chung facility.

How are lithium batteries handled on air shipments?

Consignments containing lithium cells or batteries are packed, marked and declared to the applicable requirements. Correct declaration is a condition of acceptance.

Can you ship direct to Dubai and Miami?

Yes, and those two lanes are handled by Universal at both ends, so the consignment is received by the same company that despatched it.
