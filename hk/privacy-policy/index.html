Privacy policy

This policy explains what Universal Freight (HK) Ltd does with information submitted through universalfreight.hk. It covers this website only. It does not cover the handling of commercial or operational information exchanged once you become a customer, which is governed by the terms agreed between us.

What this website collects

Enquiry and quote forms

When you submit an enquiry we collect the details you enter: your name, company, email address, telephone and WhatsApp numbers, country, and the shipment information you choose to provide, together with any message you write.

We also record the page the enquiry was sent from, the referring website, any campaign parameters in the link you arrived through, the time of submission, your IP address and your browser user-agent string. That context helps us respond appropriately and helps us detect automated abuse of the form.

Analytics

If analytics is enabled on this site, it is loaded only after you agree to it. It records how pages are used — which pages are viewed, and whether visitors start a quote form, click a phone number, click an email address or open WhatsApp.

What you type into a form is never sent to analytics. Only the fact that a submission occurred is recorded, not its contents.

Anti-spam

To prevent automated submissions we may use Cloudflare Turnstile. Where it is enabled, Cloudflare receives technical information about your browser in order to confirm that the submission is not automated.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a quotation.
  • To contact you about the enquiry you submitted.
  • To prevent automated abuse of the enquiry form.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how this website is used.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use enquiry details to send marketing you did not ask for.

Who else is involved

Enquiries are transmitted by email through a transactional email provider and delivered to a Universal mailbox. Where analytics is enabled, Google receives the analytics data described above. Where anti-spam is enabled, Cloudflare receives the verification data described above. This website is served from a hosting provider which processes standard server request logs.

Where an enquiry concerns a movement handled by another Universal office, the details may be shared with that office so it can respond.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are retained in our email systems for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the correspondence. Where an enquiry becomes a commercial relationship, the information is retained under our ordinary business records practice.

Your choices

  • You can decline analytics. If you decline, no analytics tag is loaded on this site.
  • You can ask us what information we hold about you from this website, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing info@universalfreight.hk.
  • You can contact us by telephone or email instead of using the form if you prefer not to submit information through the website.

Cookies and local storage

This website stores your analytics choice in your browser so we do not ask again on every visit, and stores campaign parameters for the duration of your visit so an enquiry can be attributed to the link you arrived through. If you agree to analytics, Google Analytics sets its own cookies. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set by this website.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy should go to info@universalfreight.hk, or to Universal Freight (HK) Ltd at the address above.
