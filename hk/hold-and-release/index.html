Hold and release in Hong Kong

Cargo received and held in secure storage, moving only on the authorised party’s written release instruction.

Overview

At the origin end of a supply chain the deadlock takes a slightly different shape. A supplier has assembled the stock and wants certainty before it leaves their control. An overseas buyer is being asked to commit against goods sitting in a city they are not in.

Holding the consignment at Kwai Chung gives both sides a fixed point. The goods are in an independent facility, counted and recorded, and they move when the authorised party instructs it.

What is included

What hold & release covers

  • Receipt into hold

    Consignments are received, verified and placed into secure storage under a hold, rather than being automatically forwarded.

  • Named release authority

    The party entitled to instruct release is recorded when the hold is placed, and release instructions are accepted only from that party.

  • Written instruction only

    Release is actioned on a written instruction. Verbal requests are not acted on.

  • Partial release

    A consignment can be released in stages where a transaction settles in tranches, with the balance remaining on hold.

  • Status confirmation

    Both the holding party and, where authorised, the counterparty can be confirmed the goods are received and held.

  • Release to a nominated party

    Goods can be released to a collecting agent, forwarded onward, or handed to a buyer’s carrier as the instruction specifies.

How it works

Step by step

Each stage produces something you can check, and each decision point stays with you.
  1. 01

    Hold instruction received

    Before or on arrival, you instruct that the consignment is to be held and name who is authorised to release it.

  2. 02

    Cargo received and verified

    The consignment is booked in, counted and — where instructed — scanned and inspected, so what is being held is documented.

  3. 03

    Held in secure storage

    Stock is stored under the hold. It is not forwarded, not released and not handed to any third party.

  4. 04

    Position confirmed

    You are confirmed the goods are in hand and held, which is normally what the counterparty is waiting on.

  5. 05

    Release instructed

    The authorised party issues a written release instruction naming what is to be released and to whom.

  6. 06

    Released or forwarded

    The consignment leaves the hold and is handed over or moved onward exactly as instructed.

Hong Kong context

Holding stock at source

Hong Kong is where a large share of Asian-sourced electronics is aggregated before export, which makes it the natural place for stock to sit while a transaction is finalised. It is close enough to suppliers that an issue can still be corrected, and it is a jurisdiction both sides of a cross-border trade tend to be comfortable with.

Because Hong Kong is a free port, stock can be received and held without the duty consequences that holding it in a destination market might create. Goods that end up being redirected have not been imported anywhere they were not meant to go.

Where a consignment has been assembled from several suppliers, the hold also serves an operational purpose: it keeps the completed order together in one place, under one reference, until it is cleared to travel.

Scope and limits

  • This is a cargo custody service, not a financial one. Universal does not hold, transmit, escrow or release funds, and takes no part in the commercial settlement between buyer and seller.
  • Universal acts on the instruction of the party recorded as the release authority. It does not adjudicate between disputing parties and cannot resolve a commercial disagreement.
  • A hold is a storage and handling arrangement. It is not a legal lien, a pledge, a bailment guarantee or a form of security over the goods.

Common questions

Hold & release — questions we are asked

Can stock be held while it is still being assembled from multiple suppliers?

Yes. Deliveries can be received progressively and held as they arrive, with the hold released only once the order is complete and the authorised instruction is given.

Can you confirm to my buyer that you are holding the goods?

Where you authorise it, the fact that a consignment has been received and is held can be confirmed to a named counterparty. Universal describes what it holds; it does not certify commercial quality or value.

Does Universal handle any part of the payment?

No. Universal holds and releases cargo only. It has no involvement in payment, financing, escrow or settlement of any kind.

Can goods be released straight into an export booking?

Yes. Release and onward forwarding can be a single instruction, so the consignment moves from hold directly into its outbound leg.
