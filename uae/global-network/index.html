Dubai, Hong Kong and Miami

Three operations positioned where the high-value electronics trade physically routes — and connected so a consignment moving between them is handled by Universal at both ends.

Network map

How the network works

Both ends of the lane

The electronics trade does not move randomly. Product originates in Asia, is aggregated and traded through a small number of hubs, and is distributed into consuming markets from there. Hong Kong, Dubai and Miami are three of the points that pattern runs through.

Universal operates in all three. That means a consignment leaving one facility is received at another by the same company, under the same reference, with the same record of what is in it — rather than being handed to an unrelated agent whose relationship with you begins the moment the cargo lands.

Common questions

About the network

Are the three offices the same company?

They are three separate legal entities, each operating in its own jurisdiction under the Universal name: Universal Logistics FZE in Dubai, Universal Freight (HK) Ltd in Hong Kong and Universal Freight & Logistics LLC in Miami. They work as a connected network.

What does it change if both ends are handled by Universal?

The consignment reference, the scan record and the handling instructions carry across. A query at the destination does not require reconstructing what happened at origin from a third party.

Can you handle a movement to a destination outside the three?

Yes. Air and sea movements are arranged worldwide through carrier and agent relationships. The three offices are where Universal handles the cargo itself.
